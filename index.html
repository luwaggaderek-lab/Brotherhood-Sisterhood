CONSTITUTION OF BROTHERHOOD AND SISTERHOOD.

MOTTO:."ONE FAMILY, SUPPORT, GROW AND SERVE. "

INTRODUCTION:

We, the members of the Brotherhood and Sisterhood Program, commit ourselves to building a family founded on love, unity, respect, trust and mutual support. We adopt this constitution to guide our activities, relationships.

ARTICLE 1:NAME OF THE GROUP BROTHERHOOD AND SISTERHOOD

ARTICLE 2a:VISION

To build a united and loving family where everyone feels valued, supported and encouraged.

2b: MISSION

To promote love, unity, spiritual growth, personal development and mutual support among members.

ARTICLE 3: OBJECTIVES

  • To promote love, peace and togetherness.
  • To support one another during difficult times.(sickness,loss,etc
  • To encourage spiritual and personal growth among members.
  • To encourage savings and economic growth among members.

ARTICLE 4: MEMBERSHIP

Membership is open to anyone who believes in the vision and values of the program and is willing to participate positively.

ARTICLE 5: RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Every member has the right to be respected, heard and supported. Members are expected to:

  • Respect one another.
  • Attend meetings and participate .
  • Maintain honesty and confidentiality.
  • Support and encourage fellow members.
  • Avoid gossip, discrimination and unnecessary conflicts.

ARTICLE 6: LEADERSHIP

The leadership shall consist of:

  • Chairperson
  • Vice Chairperson
  • General Secretary
  • Treasurer
  • Welfare Coordinator

NOTE; Leaders shall guide the organization and serve members with honesty, fairness and accountability.

ARTICLE 7: MEETINGS

  • *General meetings to be held on.....
  • *Executive meetings to be held on .......
  • *Emergency meetings can be called upon ;
  • *Members shall meet regularly as agreed. *Decisions shall be made through discussion and mutual agreement or voting when necessary.

ARTICLE 8: FINANCES

Members may agree on contributions to support group activities and welfare. All funds must be properly recorded, managed transparently and used for approved purposes.

ARTICLE 9: DISCIPLINE AND CONFLICT RESOLUTION

  • *Members shall treat one another peacefully. *Misconduct shall be addressed through counselling, warnings or other fair disciplinary measures.

ARTICLE 10: AMENDMENTS

This constitution may be changed with the approval of at least two-thirds of members present and eligible to vote.

ARTICLE 11: FINAL COMMITMENT

We commit ourselves to loving, respecting, encouraging and supporting one another. We shall stand together in good and difficult times and work towards building a strong and meaningful brotherhood and sisterhood.

MOTTO:."ONE FAMILY, SUPPORT, GROW AND SERVE. "
